# claude.ai project memory: quality-air-solutions

**Purpose & context**

Dom works on sales and marketing for Quality Air Solution (QAS), a Singapore-based company and exclusive Airsmeg distributor across Singapore, Malaysia, and Indonesia. QAS offers three products on a rental-only, fully-maintained model: the Airsmeg AS-150 electrostatic air purifier, the Airsonic-QAS evaporative air cooler, and the KOMC kitchen oil mist collector. The company's website is qas.sg (recently revamped). Dom's work spans customer-facing WhatsApp sales replies, marketing website content, competitive positioning, and sales training materials.

**Key learnings & principles**

- The AS-150 cannot win a headline price comparison against consumer brands (Dyson, Coway); defensible positioning requires framing it as a commercial-grade category, not a consumer product — hard segmentation toward commercial and professional spaces is recommended
- Rental value proposition is best framed around predictability, zero maintenance burden, and risk transfer rather than raw cost savings; the rent-vs-buy break-even is around year three
- Singapore SME behavior favors OPEX over CAPEX structures, which supports the rental model pitch
- Emotional/behavioral triggers vary by product: health anxiety and haze for AS-150, electricity cost for Airsonic, NEA compliance risk for KOMC
- For WhatsApp outreach, shorter messages generate better reply rates; including pricing upfront risks anchoring the conversation on cost rather than the trial offer
- Operations-oriented (commercial) audiences respond better to practical benefits (washable plates, included servicing) than technical filtration specs
- Ending customer messages with a qualifying question about space type and size is a recommended tactic to move toward a site assessment
- Accuracy flags matter: Dom appreciates when unsupported claims or spec discrepancies are surfaced before content is published (e.g., the "1.6-minute room clearance" claim, 66W vs 15W discrepancy, ozone risk framing)
- The "~30× finer than HEPA" framing is approved; ozone references are omitted from all customer-facing materials

**Approach & patterns**

- Dom prefers Claude to produce concrete deliverables proactively and ask targeted clarifying questions rather than wait for direction
- Iterative refinement is standard: drafts go through multiple rounds of shortening and focusing before being finalized
- WhatsApp replies are often split into multiple messages by topic for readability
- Outputs are regularly converted into sales tools (objection-handling cards, comparison calculators, formatted decks)
