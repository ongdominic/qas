## Competitive positioning

- [stated] The AS-150 cannot win a headline price comparison against consumer brands (Dyson, Coway); defensible positioning requires framing it as a commercial-grade category, not a consumer product
- [stated] Hard segmentation toward commercial and professional spaces is the recommended approach
- [stated] Rental value proposition is best framed around predictability, zero maintenance burden, and risk transfer rather than raw cost savings
- [stated] Rent-vs-buy break-even is around year three
- [stated] Singapore SME behavior favors OPEX over CAPEX structures, which supports the rental model pitch

## Emotional and behavioral triggers by product

- [stated] AS-150: health anxiety and haze
- [stated] Airsonic: electricity cost
- [stated] KOMC: NEA compliance risk

## Customer messaging

- [stated] For WhatsApp outreach, shorter messages generate better reply rates
- [stated] Including pricing upfront risks anchoring the conversation on cost rather than the trial offer
- [stated] Operations-oriented (commercial) audiences respond better to practical benefits (washable plates, included servicing) than technical filtration specs
- [stated] Ending customer messages with a qualifying question about space type and size is a recommended tactic to move toward a site assessment
- [stated] WhatsApp replies are often split into multiple messages by topic for readability

## Claim accuracy

- [stated] Unsupported claims and spec discrepancies should be surfaced before content is published — past examples include the "1.6-minute room clearance" claim, a 66W vs 15W discrepancy, and ozone risk framing
- [stated] The "~30× finer than HEPA" framing is approved
- [stated] Ozone references are omitted from all customer-facing materials
