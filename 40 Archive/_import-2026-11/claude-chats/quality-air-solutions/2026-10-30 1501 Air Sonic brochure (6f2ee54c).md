Before I build it, a few things need answers.

**Format**
1. Which size and fold? The AS-150 catalogue is a multi-page A3 spread. The Airsonic flyer is a 2-page A4. For a brochure I'd suggest an A4 bifold (4 pages) or a trifold.
2. Will it be printed, sent as a PDF on WhatsApp, or both?
3. English only, or bilingual EN/CN like the AS-150 catalogue?

**Claims to check (the current flyer has problems)**
4. **"Cools air to 24–26°C".** Evaporative cooling works less well in humid air. In Singapore it usually brings the temperature down by about 3–6°C, so it doesn't reach a fixed 24°C. Can we say "drops temperature by X°C" instead? Do you have any measured site data?
5. **"Dehumidifier effect".** Evaporative coolers add moisture to the air, so this claim could backfire with a technical buyer. Is it from the manufacturer spec sheet? If not, I'd drop it.
6. **"3X Layer Technology".** What are the three layers? Without that, it's just a label.
7. **"$0 lifetime maintenance".** This only holds under the lease. I'd phrase it as "maintenance included while you rent".
8. **800 sq ft coverage.** That seems low for 20,000 m³/h. Please confirm the number with the supplier.
9. **"Made in Singapore".** Your homepage says "Made in Taiwan & Singapore". Which is correct for Airsonic?

**Specs I don't have**
10. Power draw (W), voltage, water tank capacity, water use per hour, dimensions, weight, noise level, and any certifications.

**Commercial**
11. Do we show pricing, or keep it enquiry-led like the purifier? What are the minimum lease term and the delivery and setup lead time?
12. Contact details: the flyer has 6665 1808 and accounts@qas.sg, but elsewhere you use +65 8188 9998 and sales@qas.sg. Which should go on it?

**Proof (this would help the most)**
13. Any of these: a client name or logo, a site where it's deployed, before and after temperature readings, or a monthly electricity cost comparison against aircon for a typical warehouse. Electricity cost is the buyer's main trigger, so a single worked example in dollars would be the strongest page in the brochure.
14. Better product and on-site photos. The current ones are mostly stock.

If you only have time for a few, answer 1, 4, 10 and 13. I'll draft with placeholders for the rest.

Two small fixes also needed in the current flyer: "maintainence" and "Responsibiity" are misspelled.

To view the PDFs I made two preview images and accidentally saved them in your QAS folder. I can't delete files there, so I moved them into a `_to_delete` folder. You can bin that folder.
