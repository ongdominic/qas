# Growth: rules for Claude

## What this is
QAS marketing and growth: positioning, brochures and collateral, website, market research, growth plan.
Website work runs in `10 Projects/website-content/`. Facts: `30 Resources/qas-product-facts.md`.

## Rules
- Segment hard toward commercial and professional spaces. Triggers: Air Purifier = health and haze;
  Airsonic = electricity cost; KOMC = NEA compliance.
- Singapore SMEs prefer OPEX over CAPEX: lead with the rental model.
- No prices in brochures or on the website; conversion runs through enquiry and site assessment.
- Surface unsupported claims and spec mismatches before anything is published (see the claims list in the facts file).
- Dom wants concrete drafts proactively, with targeted questions, then several rounds of trimming.
- Collateral is built in Claude Design (A4 PDF, English first) so Dom can drop in photos.
