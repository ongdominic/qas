# Sales: rules for Claude

## What this is
QAS customer sales: enquiries, WhatsApp replies, quotes and rental proposals, key accounts.
Facts and prices: `30 Resources/qas-product-facts.md`.

## Rules
- WhatsApp is the main channel. Keep messages short and punchy (shorter gets more replies); split by topic into
  several messages when needed; end with a qualifying question about space type and size to move toward a
  site assessment (unless Dom asks for no closing line).
- Don't lead with price: it anchors the talk on cost. Pricing goes in only when asked or for a proposal.
- Operations and commercial audiences: practical benefits (included servicing, washable plates) over filtration specs.
- Position as commercial grade, not consumer: the AS-150 can't win a headline price fight with Dyson or Coway.
  Sell the rental on predictability, zero maintenance and risk transfer.
- Free trial: no trial on short-term (3-month) leases. Trial length is unsettled (14 days in the playbook, 7 days per Dom in Aug).
- Name the product "Air Purifier". No ozone references. Use +65 8188 9998 and sales@qas.sg.
- Cold emails: aim for a named facilities or operations manager rather than a generic inbox.
- Never contact customers without Dom's go-ahead.
