# QAS: company and product facts

Check here before writing any customer-facing copy. Amounts SGD.

## Company
- Quality Air Solution (QAS), Singapore. Exclusive distributor of Euro Asia's **Airsmeg** brand for Singapore, Malaysia and Indonesia.
- Brand promise: **"Your air, our responsibility."** Website: qas.sg.
- Customer-facing contacts: **+65 8188 9998, sales@qas.sg** (use these, not the flyer's 6665 1808 / accounts@qas.sg).
- Model: **rental only, fully maintained.** No upfront cost; servicing and maintenance included; volume discounts for multiple units.
- Reference client: Yi Hui Metals rents the Airsonic-QAS (photos can be taken there).

**Naming:** always "Air Purifier", never "Market Air Purifier".

### Air Purifier (AS-150)
- 5-stage filtration: pre-filter, electrostatic precipitator, active carbon, TiO₂ photocatalytic, UV-C.
- 99.9% particle capture down to 0.01 μm; "~30× finer than HEPA" is approved framing.
- About 15 W typical power (not 66 W); washable electrostatic plates; smart PM2.5 auto-sensor.
- Works for cigarette smoke.

### Airsonic-QAS
- Three layers of cooling through QAS's own fin design, which also traps dust.
- Blow-out air about 24 to 26°C, as measured at the right fan speed (say "around").
- Don't claim a coverage area (depends on site). Claude softened "dehumidifier effect", "zero greenhouse gas" (→ "no CFCs or HFCs") and "$0 lifetime maintenance" (→ "monthly servicing included"): Dom to confirm.
- Uses: manufacturing plants, warehouses, event venues, farms; outdoor units.
